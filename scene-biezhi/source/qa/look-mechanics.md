# Look mechanics — 稀音·别枝

- Keep both boots, lower skirt, and the character's center of mass anchored to the same baseline in every direction.
- The golden pupils lead the motion. Eyelids and brows follow, then the face and neck turn slightly. Do not rotate the whole sprite.
- The wide sleeves, mustard sash, and compact folded olive-green parasol remain attached and lag only subtly behind the head turn.
- Preserve all asymmetry: dark floral hairpin and short side ponytail stay on anatomical left; the purple floral motif and olive gradient stay on the anatomical-left sleeve and hem; the sash and tassels never swap sides.
- Keep the same scale, proportions, palette, lighting, expression, and silhouette as the approved standard rows.
- Cardinal landmarks: `000` looks clearly upward; `090` looks clearly toward screen-right; `180` looks clearly downward toward the obi; `270` looks clearly toward screen-left.
- Intermediate directions advance clockwise in even 22.5-degree steps. Each adjacent frame must be visibly distinct while remaining a smooth progression.
- Keep the cyan chroma background flat and uniform. No labels, arrows, scenery, detached decorations, or opened parasol.
